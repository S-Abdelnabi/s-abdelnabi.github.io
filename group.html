COMPASS Research Group

COoperative Machine intelligence for People-Aligned Safe Systems

The COMPASS group at the ELLIS Institute Tübingen and the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems investigates how to build AI systems that are safe, aligned with human values, and robust against adversarial manipulation. We work on broad topics in A(G)I safety and security: interpretability, reasoning, evals, contextual integrity, agentic risks and opportunities, multi-agent dynamics, agents with long-term memory, self-improving agents, (deceptive) alignment, situational awareness, manipulation, and deception.
